The areas Dan's follow-ups usually fall into, so tasks are worded sensibly:

- Research: papers, preprints, reviewers, data and methods.
- School: coursework, assignments, instructors, registration and study partners.
- Work: the day job and its colleagues and clients; a company or product being built, with its customers, partners and investors; a job search, with its recruiters, hiring managers, applications and interviews.
- Writing: articles and newsletters, interviews given, and sources offered.
- Community: clubs, professional societies, conferences and volunteering.
- Personal: family, home and errands.
